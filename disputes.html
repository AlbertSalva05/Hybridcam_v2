When something goes wrong, escrow still holds the money.

There are three ways a deal can go wrong. Each one has a fixed rule, so nobody has to argue in a chat thread.

The three cases

  • Fails inspectionThe rider stops, the unit stays with the seller and escrow refunds the buyer in full. The platform absorbs the rider fee.
  • Seal broken on arrivalRefuse the OTP. Escrow keeps holding, and a courier claim opens with the two seal photos as evidence.
  • Fault found laterYou have 48 hours after delivery to report anything the twelve checks could not catch. Inside that window, escrow can be taken back from the payout.

How to open a dispute

  1. Open the order on the tracking page and choose Report a problem.
  2. Describe the fault and add a photo or short video.
  3. The support desk replies on the channel you picked and asks the seller for their side.

How disputes affect sellers

A dispute resolved in the seller's favour has no effect. One upheld against the seller pauses new listings for seven days, and the reason is shown on their profile so buyers can judge it.
