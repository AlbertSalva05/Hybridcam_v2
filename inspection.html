Twelve checks, on camera, before money moves.

The rider runs every check on a live three-way call with the buyer and seller watching. The clip is attached to the order, so the result is evidence and not an opinion.

Body and power

  1. Power on from a charged battery, with the battery door and contacts shown.
  2. Shutter fired at 1/1000, 1/125 and 1 second so the buyer hears all three.
  3. Shutter count read from the camera menu or a counting app, and compared with the listing.
  4. Serial number shown on the body and matched to the number the seller logged.

Image path

  1. Sensor check: a frame of a white wall at f/16, reviewed on the call for spots.
  2. Lens glass lit with a phone torch from both ends, looking for haze, fungus and scratches.
  3. Autofocus on a near and a far subject, in single and continuous modes.
  4. Screen and viewfinder shown on a plain frame to reveal dead or stuck pixels.

Controls and storage

  1. Every dial and button pressed once, including the ones the buyer names.
  2. Card slot: one photo written, played back and deleted.
  3. Battery seated and charged to the level the seller declared.
  4. Body photographed on all six sides so any wear is on record.

If a check fails

The rider stops. The unit stays with the seller and the buyer's escrow is refunded. The clip shows which check failed, so the seller can fix the fault or relist with an honest description. See the dispute window for faults found after delivery.
