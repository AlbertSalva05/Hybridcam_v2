You are paid when the buyer enters the OTP.

Escrow releases at the door. The fee depends on how you sell, and it is taken from the payout, never charged up front.

Fees per cleared sale

  • Standard, 6%Rider inspection, escrow, the seal kit and dispute handling are included.
  • Verified shop, 4.5%For sellers with 10 or more cleared sales. Adds priority rider dispatch and a shop page.
  • Consignment, 9%Drop the body at the Manila lab. The team photographs it, lists it and handles every meet-up.

When the money lands

  • OTP entered before 4 PM on a banking day: transfer the same day.
  • OTP entered after 4 PM, or on a weekend or holiday: transfer the next banking morning.
  • Failed inspection: nothing is paid and nothing is charged to you.

Worked example

A Fujifilm X-S10 listed at ₱44,000 on the Standard plan pays out ₱41,360 after the 6% fee.
