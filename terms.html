Terms and conditions

The rules every buyer and seller agrees to when an order starts. This design build shows the intended terms; a lawyer should review them before launch.

Last updated 18 October 2026

1. The order is the agreement

A deal on hybrid.cam is the order record: the unit, its serial number, the price and the meet point. Side deals made in chat are not covered by escrow.

2. Escrow

The buyer's payment is held from rider booking until the buyer enters the delivery OTP. hybrid.cam releases it to the seller only after the inspection checklist passes and the OTP is entered.

3. Seller duties

  • List only units you own and can hand over.
  • Describe faults honestly. A serial number that does not match the listing cancels the sale.
  • Be at the meet point in the agreed window.

4. Buyer duties

  • Watch the inspection call or accept its result.
  • Check the seal before entering the OTP.
  • Report later faults inside the 48-hour dispute window.

5. Fees

Seller fees are listed on the payout schedule. The rider fee is quoted before booking and shown on the order.

6. Limits

Transit cover is limited to the escrowed value of the unit. hybrid.cam is not responsible for faults that appear after the dispute window closes.

7. Changes

If these terms change, the date above changes too, and orders already in progress keep the terms they started with.
