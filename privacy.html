Privacy policy

What this site keeps, where it keeps it and how to remove it. This is a design build: nothing you type is sent to a server.

Last updated 18 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us from this build. It runs no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels, and it sets no cookies.

What stays in your browser

A few items are saved in this browser's storage, on this device only:

  • Display settings: theme and motion.
  • A note that you closed the rewards popup.
  • Your cookie choices.
  • A staff sign-in for the admin pages, kept only until you close the tab.

You choose what is kept in cookie settings. Essential items are always kept because they remember your choices.

Forms and orders

The inquiry form, delivery wizard and ID check are simulations. What you type stays on the page and is gone when you reload.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The tracking map loads MapLibre from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those services see your IP address when your browser fetches the files.

Delete your data

  • On the Settings page, choose Clear saved settings.
  • In cookie settings, turn off what you no longer want kept. The saved copy is deleted straight away.
  • Or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
